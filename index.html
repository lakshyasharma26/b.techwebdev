Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to design and launch a website?
Standard 3 to 6 page business websites are typically delivered within 5 to 10 working days upon receiving assets. More complex e-commerce platforms and custom CRM systems generally take 2 to 4 weeks depending on the technical integration scope.
Do you provide hosting and domain name support?
Yes. We guide you through purchasing your preferred domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap) and assist with setting up high-speed cloud hosting (Vercel, Netlify, Render, AWS). All server accounts remain 100% under your ownership.
Can I update the content on my website myself later?
Absolutely. Depending on your package, we can integrate a simple content management panel or headless CMS so you can easily modify texts, add products, upload blogs, or manage user data without touching raw code.
What happens after the project is deployed?
Every build comes with 30 to 45 days of free post-launch bug fixing and support. We also provide ongoing monthly website maintenance, security auditing, and SEO retainer packages for continuous peace of mind.
